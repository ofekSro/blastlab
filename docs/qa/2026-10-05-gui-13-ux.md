## 3. Findings (medium and above - each becomes a task)

No high findings. The batch adds no threading change and no path that loses input. The
export note is computed on the GUI thread before the worker starts (`ui/exports.py:111-117`).

**F1 - medium - Chain, default route - the default chain opens with two warnings and
two `!` rows.**
- Where: `ui/chain_panel.py` (no seed), implement-A §A1 and its open question 1.
- What the user sees: a new user opening Chain sees `⚠` on cards 2 and 3 and the warn
  glyph on both `Domain & mesh` rows before touching anything. B2's glyph makes this more
  visible, not less. The batch goal ("opens without the F45 warning") is not met.
- Fix: the owner decides implement-A open question 1. The recommended combination is
  (c) for Lz, which keeps 1.5 m and relies on F45's advice, plus re-recording
  `ui_chain_wiring.json` so the stage-1 radius seed of 1.5 m lands. Card 2 is then clean.
  Card 3 stays warned by design under (c), so the F45 text should carry the whole
  message. If the owner prefers a clean card 3, option (b) is a numbers decision (D2).

**F2 - medium - 01 Setup, Gauges table vs Checks - Where can name a different wall than
the warning.**
- Where: `ui/scaled_distance.where_text` (`:212-235`) uses the nearest wall, and only the
  suffix comes from `drop_rule`. Implement-A §"Engineering issues" 2 records the case.
- What the user sees: Checks says `G3 (1.63, 0.30) m on the face of Wall 1 - will be
  dropped`, while the same row's Where says `1 cm in front of Wall 2 (dropped)`. Two
  statements on one screen disagree about which wall to move.
- Fix: return the wall number from `drop_rule` (it already has it). When the gauge is
  dropped, Where reads `<n> cm from Wall <drop wall> (dropped)`, so it names the same wall

**F3 - medium - 01 Setup and Chain - a gauge that will be dropped is not marked where
gauges are edited.**
- Where: `setup_panel.py:851-857` (the Where cell is `muted`; the outside case is blank)
  and `:609-610` (the tree has `error` / `ok` only). The chain stage tree has the same

- What the user sees:
  - A gauge outside the domain has an empty Where cell.
  - `inside Wall 1` and `... (dropped)` are in the same grey as an ordinary `12 cm in

  - The `Gauges` tree node keeps its ✓ while Checks says a gauge will be dropped. This
    is the same gap that B2 fixed for F45 on `Domain & mesh`, left open on the next node.

  - Where reads `outside the domain (dropped)` for an out-of-domain gauge.

**F4 - medium - 02 Run, selected-gauge card - middle elision keeps "selected" and cuts
the location.**
- Where: `run_gauges.py:532` (`ElideMiddle`) and `:570-574` (text `Z … · <where> ·

- What the user sees: implement-B measured the header at about 130 px at 1100 × 720. The

  - Use `ElideRight`, so the order of importance (Z, then Where) is what survives.
  - Write `Z 1.23 m/kg^1/3`, or reuse `scaled_distance.LABEL`'s unit.
  - B1's pin "header and peak rects do not overlap" stays valid.

## 6. Parking (low - listed, not tasked)

- **F45 advice and the cell cap.** Following "Raise Lz to at least 4.0 m" gives a wired
  stage 3 a `Large grid ... expect hours` warning until the startup hardware probe
  (`app.py:454`) finishes. A charge-start 3D at 4 m errors on the unprobed 4 GB plan
  (implement-A §A1). The window is transient and the probe normally finishes.
- **F45 is a 3-clause, about 230-character line.** A shorter lead (`Burst z 2.0 m ≥ Lz
  1.5 m: the 3D box cuts the burst off.`) with the advice as a second sentence would
  scan faster in the Checks list.
- **The figure titles switch format on a drop.** `Gauge 4  (x, y) m` becomes
  `G4 (x, y) m → cell (x, y)`, and the cell has no unit. The format change is deliberate
  (F42 byte rule). The missing ` m` after the cell is not.
- **The Qt and Tk footnotes differ for the same run.** Qt gives
  `G2 dropped by the solver (inside Wall 1)`; Tk gives
  `Not shown: G2 - dropped by the solver`. The Tk wording is acceptable because the
  reason is unknown there.
- **The footnote does not wrap.** With several drops, Qt's `·`-joined note can be wider
  than the figure, and `supxlabel` will clip it in the PNG. `wrap=True` or a joined
  `\n` fixes it.
- **The coordinate format differs across surfaces.** Tk's gauge label uses `->` and
  3 decimals, while Qt and the figure use `→` and 2 decimals.
- **The 1D Checks line reads `G1 (5.00) m outside the domain`.** A radius in parentheses
  reads oddly; `G1 at r 5.00 m` would be clearer.
- **The export-title hint is hover-only.** It appears only on the All-plots and Report
  tooltips. That is acceptable, and it is better than the canvas-wide tooltip it
  replaced.
- **B2's click-to-focus link is deferred (D8).** The Checks line still cannot take the
  user to `Lz`.
- **`drop_rule` failures are silent.** When it swallows a layout exception, Where loses
  its ` (dropped)` without saying so. The Checks error for the bad input already
  explains it.
- **The S2-1 glyph is on card 2's Domain row.** One of the two fixes (the 1D radius) is
  on stage 1, and the text says so.
