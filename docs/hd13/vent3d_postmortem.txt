HOW THE GAUGES WERE READ
  `--load` (9b5212f) reads case.json and postProcessing/gauges/0/{p,U,rho}
  and nothing else. It goes through reader.read_probes(case_unc) - the
  call the live path's backend.finalize_3d makes - and packs the result
  into finalize_3d's gauge dict. No field, no reconstructPar, no solver.
  postmortem.load_run was not used: it reads all 748 written times and
  may reconstruct into the run.
  The probes are blastProbes, sampled every solver step, with
  adjustLocations yes and the default `cell` interpolation. Each sample
  is therefore the raw value of the cell findCell elects (blastFoam User
  Guide v6.0, sec. 15.1, pp. 122-123). 3395 samples, 0 to 30 ms.
