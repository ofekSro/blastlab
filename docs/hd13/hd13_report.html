What this is. An HD 1.3 material deflagrates rather than detonates. Entering it as a "TNT equivalent" does not merely change the magnitude - it moves the answer to the opposite corner of the pressure-impulse diagram, because the load is quasi-static rather than impulsive. The blast path in this repo models detonation and does not apply.

Regime is the point. A detonation puts a wall in the impulsive corner, where only the impulse matters. A confined deflagration puts it in the quasi-static one, where only the peak pressure does. A quasi-static pressure that merely reaches the element's ultimate resistance fails it, however slowly it arrives.
