## 2. Triage fixes on the real platform (`QT_QPA_PLATFORM` unset, 1100 × 720)

### F29: the top bar (`894f70e`). FIXED by the verifier (F37)

The probe (`v10b_topbar_probe.py`, 216 checks, both themes, both solver modes) checks
these things in each state:

- Nothing goes past the Win32 client rect.
- The pills, the chip and the marker keep their whole text.
- The name shown fits its button.
- An elided name means there is no room left: the stretch is 0, or the button is at
  its 200 px maximum.
- An elided name has the whole name as its tooltip, and `TopBar.case_name()` returns
  the whole name.
- A whole name has no tooltip.

The probe also covers a 1100-1500 px sweep (`wall_A_blastfoam` is whole from 1240 px,
where the stretch becomes positive), Save as (long name) → Open (short) → Load into
Setup (`untitled`) → Open (long), a theme switch both ways, and an app font ×1.25 and
back.
